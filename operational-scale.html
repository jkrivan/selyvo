Questions people ask

Can a customer approve time sheets on SELYVO?

Yes. The customer’s approver sees each time sheet with its days and what was done, and approves it or returns it with a comment.

Do I need MailChat?

No, it is optional. It gives your business a SELYVO mailbox for writing to partners, and your own e-mail address is never shown.

Can freelancers use SELYVO?

Yes. A freelancer joins as a one-person business, by invitation like any other, and works with the same tools.
