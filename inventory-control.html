Questions people ask

Can a supplier deliver in several parts?

Yes. Each part has its delivery note and the customer confirms what arrived. The order stays open until everything is in.

Who can see an order’s messages?

The people working on the order at both businesses. A message goes out only when you press Send.

Do orders follow approval rules?

Yes. Your business decides who approves which orders, and the order waits until they do.
