Step 1 / 4 · Received

An invoice arrives.

By e-mail to invoice@market.selyvo.com, as a file, or on SELYVO — read by AI and matched to its business.

Step 2 / 4 · Checked

Every field the law asks for, your details, and each line against the order and what you received. If you choose, one that fails goes back to its supplier by itself.

Approved invoices wait to be paid, soonest due first.

Questions people ask

Can I send invoices made in my own accounting?

Yes. Upload the file: SELYVO reads it and sends it to the customer with its order.

Can we bring our past invoices?

Yes. Your past invoices are read by AI, and their suppliers can be invited to SELYVO.

How is VAT handled across borders?

SELYVO applies the rate, the reverse charge or the exemption that fits the two countries and prints the wording the law requires.

What happens to an invoice that does not match its order?

It waits for you with what the check found. If you choose, it goes back to its supplier by itself, with what to correct, and the corrected invoice takes its place.
