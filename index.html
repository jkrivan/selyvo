How it works

From an idea to a paid invoice.

Seven steps, one record. Follow a purchase through SELYVO Market — on its real screens.

Scroll to follow it step by step

Step 1 / 7 · Request

An idea becomes a request

Describe what you need, or drop a specification, a tender or a bill of quantities: AI fills in the title, the category, the lines, the delivery and the budget — you choose what to keep.

Good to know A request can ask suppliers to answer a questionnaire first.

Step 2 / 7 · Suppliers

The right suppliers, verified

Invite your own suppliers, businesses already on SELYVO, or let AI find more — each with its rating, its categories and where it delivers.

Good to know Before AI searches, the button says what it will cost: about 0.60 EUR.

Step 3 / 7 · Offers

Offers, side by side

Each supplier answers on SELYVO. You see who will send an offer, compare the offers line by line, and choose the best.

Good to know Suppliers found by AI are invited by e-mail and can join SELYVO to send their offer.

Step 4 / 7 · Order

The order

The offer you accept becomes the purchase order. The supplier accepts it, and both sides follow one status: open, to receive, received, invoiced, paid.

Good to know Each supplier decides how it is paid: up front by card, or by invoice after delivery.

Step 5 / 7 · Received

Delivered and received

A delivery note arrives with the goods. You confirm what arrived — in full or in part — and the order moves on by itself; goods you keep in stock add themselves.

Good to know Something wrong? Say so on the delivery note, and the supplier is told at once.

Step 6 / 7 · Checked

The invoice, checked at SELYVO

The supplier invoices from the order. Before anyone looks at it, SELYVO checks every field the law asks for, your details, and each line against the order and what you received.

Good to know If you choose, one that fails goes back to its supplier by itself, with what to correct.

Step 7 / 7 · Paid

Paid — and marked paid

Pay approved invoices straight from your own Revolut Business account, on their due dates or now. When the money arrives, both sides see the invoice paid.

Good to know Within the limit you set, payments go at once; above it, they wait for your approvers in Revolut.

Questions people ask

Who can join SELYVO Market?

Businesses and freelancers invited by a member. Ask a customer or supplier who is on SELYVO to invite you. If you do not know anyone on SELYVO yet, write to us.

How does SELYVO know a business is real?

Each company is found in its business register, checked against sanctions lists and qualified with SELYVO’s questions before it can trade. Customers’ reviews follow its orders.

What does it cost?

SELYVO’s fees are paid from a prepaid budget as they fall due. You add funds by card or bank transfer and see every charge in your account.

Does it work with our accounting?

Yes. Connect your accounting or ERP in Connected systems and choose what it carries. Invoices made in your own accounting can be sent through SELYVO from a file, and every invoice e-mail carries its PDF and its ISDOC file.

Is there a phone app?

SELYVO works in the browser on a computer, a tablet or a phone. Every screen adapts to a phone.
