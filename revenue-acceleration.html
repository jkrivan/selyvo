Questions people ask

Who sees my listings?

Members of SELYVO Market, in the regions you deliver to. They see your member prices and your rating.

Can I sell to a company that is not on SELYVO yet?

Invite it. Once it has joined, it can order from you and receive your invoices on SELYVO.

How do requests reach me?

Requests published in your categories and countries appear in Sales. A buyer can also invite you by name.
