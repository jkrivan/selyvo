Step 1 / 5 · Request

Describe what you need.

Type it, or drop a specification, a tender or a bill of quantities — AI fills in the title, the category, the lines, the delivery and the budget.

Step 2 / 5 · Suppliers

Choose who quotes.

Your own suppliers, businesses on SELYVO, or more found by AI. Before AI searches, the button says what it will cost: about 0.60 EUR.

Step 3 / 5 · Offers

Offers come in.

Each supplier says whether it will send an offer, and the offers arrive on the request by the date you set.

Step 4 / 5 · Award

Pick the best.

Compare the offers line by line or by lot, and award the best one.

Step 5 / 5 · Order

The order goes out.

The offer you accept becomes the purchase order. Both sides follow it to delivery and invoice.

Buy

Everything a purchase needs

01

Requests from a file

Drop a specification: AI finds the lots, the lines with quantities and units, and the questions for suppliers. Nothing is filled in before you check it.

02

Lots for products and services

Put the materials in one lot and the transport or assembly in another. Each lot has its own budget and is awarded on its own.

03

You choose who quotes

Any supplier in the category from the countries you pick, or only those you invite by name. Your questionnaires go with the request.

04

Offers compared side by side

By lot, by offer or line by line: price, delivery and terms next to each other, with the lowest price and the earliest delivery marked.

05

Purchase orders from the offer

Award each lot and create the purchase order from the winning offer. The supplier accepts it on SELYVO and you follow it to delivery and invoice.

06

Everyday buying from the Store

For regular items, order straight from verified suppliers’ listings at member prices, or collect them in the basket.

The AI negotiator Coming next

Set what matters, your target and your limit. The AI writes to the supplier in its language from your SELYVO mailbox, says that it is an AI, and asks you before anything is agreed. In the video it agrees €8,469.60 on an offer of €8,894.40, 4.8% less.

Questions people ask

Can a supplier that is not on SELYVO send an offer?

Invite it by e-mail with your request. It joins SELYVO before it can send an offer, so every offer comes from a verified business.

Do suppliers see my budget?

No. A lot’s budget is for your own planning. Suppliers see it only if you choose to share it.

Can I buy without a request?

Yes. Order straight from a listing in the Store, at the member price the supplier sets.
